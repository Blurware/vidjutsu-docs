VidJutsu Cloud is a video editor your agent drives. Every step is a call, so an agent can do the whole job; when you want to watch the cut and say what to change, there's a [Mac editor](/editor/index) that works with your VidJutsu Cloud account.

1. **Upload** your takes, music and stills. Files go straight to storage, up to 50 GB each.
2. **Edit** a project's timeline with plain JSON operations: place clips, trim, cut pauses, set fades and levels. Every edit is a new revision, so nothing is lost and retries are safe.
3. **Render** the timeline to 1080p or 4K on parallel cloud machines. Ranges that didn't change since the last render are reused, so a small edit re-renders in seconds.

## What it does and doesn't do

VidJutsu edits and renders footage you bring. It doesn't generate video, and it doesn't post to social networks.

- **Inputs:** video (phone footage, screen recordings, ProRes, anything common), audio and still images. Footage is stored as you uploaded it and converted at render time, only the parts a render uses.
- **Timeline:** video tracks drawn bottom to top (a screen, a camera over it, graphics over both), any number of audio tracks mixed together, clip gain, linear fades, screen and camera [layouts](/guides/layouts) with shapes and smooth moves, and cutting pauses across every track at once.
- **Planning and history:** a [shot list](/guides/shot-list) of scenes and shots with each take filed under its scene, word search across every file, and [history with undo](/guides/agents#undo-and-history) of any single change.
- **Audio:** level clips to a target loudness, and [Studio Sound](/guides/studio-sound) to clean every voice.
- **Checking the work:** [stills of the cut](/guides/look-and-check) exactly as the render draws them, and measurements of gaps, black, silence and loudness.
- **Coming from Descript:** open a Descript [timeline export](/guides/import-descript) as a project.
- **Working together:** agents and people in the same project, with [live updates](/guides/live-updates), comments, and what each agent is working on.
- **Output:** an MP4 at 1920x1080 or 3840x2160, 30 fps, with a download link.
