VidJutsu Cloud is a video editor your agent drives. Every step is a call, so an agent can do the whole job; when you want to watch the cut and say what to change, there's a free [Mac editor](/editor/index).

1. **Upload** your takes, music and stills. Files go straight to storage, up to 50 GB each.
2. **Edit** a project's timeline with plain JSON operations: place clips, trim, cut pauses, set fades and levels. Every edit is a new revision, so nothing is lost and retries are safe.
3. **Render** the timeline to 1080p or 4K on parallel cloud machines. Ranges that didn't change since the last render are reused, so a small edit re-renders in seconds.

## What it does and doesn't do

VidJutsu edits and renders footage you bring. It doesn't generate video, and it doesn't post to social networks.

- **Inputs:** video (phone footage, screen recordings, ProRes, anything common), audio and still images. Footage is stored as you uploaded it and converted at render time, only the parts a render uses.
- **Timeline:** video tracks (the first is the picture, tracks above it hold still-image overlays), any number of audio tracks mixed together, clip gain, linear fades, picture-in-picture layouts, and cutting pauses across every track at once.
- **Audio:** level clips to a target loudness, and studio-sound cleanup for voice tracks with an optional music bed.
- **Output:** an MP4 at 1920x1080 or 3840x2160, 30 fps, with a download link.
