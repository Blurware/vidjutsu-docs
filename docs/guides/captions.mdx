---
title: "Captions"
description: "Burn captions into a render, or get them as an SRT or WebVTT file. Both come from the words on the edited timeline, so they follow every cut."
---

Captions come from the words heard on the edited timeline: the transcripts of the sounds on its audio tracks, cut the way the timeline cuts them. Delete a sentence and its caption goes too. Move a clip and its captions move with it.

Every sound on the timeline needs a transcript first ([make one](/guides/uploads#previews-and-transcripts) with `make_transcript`). While one is missing, both calls below answer 400 and name the media, and a render is refused before anything is charged.

## Burn captions into a render

Send `captions` with the render:

```sh
curl -s -X POST https://api.vidjutsu.ai/projects/launch-film/renders \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"height": 1080, "captions": {"position": "bottom", "size": "medium"}}'
```

| Field | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `on` | `true` | Sending `captions` turns them on. `false` renders without them. |
| `position` | `bottom` | `bottom` or `top`. |
| `size` | `medium` | `small`, `medium` or `large`: 4, 5 or 6.4% of the frame height. |
| `background` | `solid` | `solid` puts a dark box behind each line. `none` is white text with a thin dark edge. |

There is one plain style: white sans text. Captions cost nothing extra; the render is billed by its box time as usual ([pricing](/pricing)).

## Get the captions as a file

```sh
curl -s "https://api.vidjutsu.ai/projects/launch-film/captions?format=vtt" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $KEY"
```

```json
{
  "project": "launch-film", "branch": "main", "revision": "9f2c...",
  "format": "vtt", "name": "launch-film.vtt",
  "cues": [
    {"start": {"ticks": 36, "rate": 30}, "end": {"ticks": 102, "rate": 30},
     "text": "This is the launch film.", "lines": ["This is the launch film."]}
  ],
  "file": "WEBVTT\n\n00:00:01.200 --> 00:00:03.400\nThis is the launch film.\n"
}
```

`format` is `srt` (the default) or `vtt`. `file` is the whole file's text and `name` a file name for it. `cues` are the same captions with exact timeline times, and they're the cues a render with `captions` burns in. Send `revision` or `branch` for another point in history. This call is free and doesn't render anything.

The CLI: `vidjutsu render launch-film --captions` (with `--captions-position top`, `--captions-size small|large` and `--captions-background none`), and `vidjutsu captions launch-film --format vtt --out launch-film.vtt`. The SDK: `render(project, {captions: {}})` and `captions(project, {format})`. MCP: `render` with `captions`, and `captions`.
