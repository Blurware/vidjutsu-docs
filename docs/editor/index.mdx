VidJutsu Editor is a native Mac app for VidJutsu Cloud. Plan the video, record it, and cut it with your agent: the agent edits through VidJutsu Cloud, and you see every change land as it happens, with Undo on each one.

## What it does

- **Plan with the shot list.** Lay out the video as scenes and shots, each with talking points, a rough script line and a key visual. A sidebar shows how many shots of each scene are recorded. See [Plan and record with the shot list](/guides/shot-list).
- **Record a scene or a shot.** Record your screen, camera and microphone. Screen and camera are saved as two files, and each take is filed under its scene and shot as soon as you stop. Nothing goes on the timeline until you or your agent put it there.
- **Cut by the script.** Read the cut as a script next to the video. Select words and press Delete to cut them; pauses show in the script, so you can cut those too.
- **Lay out screen and camera.** Pick a layout under the player (a circle in the corner, side by side, camera only and more), drag and resize the camera on the video, pick its shape, and save your own presets. Moves between layouts glide. See [Screen and camera layouts](/guides/layouts).
- **Studio Sound.** One switch cleans every voice in the project. It shows the price before you turn it on, about 1.5 cents a voice minute. See [Studio Sound](/guides/studio-sound).
- **Watch your agent work.** The Activity column shows each change as it lands, who made it, and what an agent says it's about to do, whether it's the built-in agent or one of your own over [MCP](/mcp). Press ⌘Z to undo the last change, or undo any single change from history.
- **Find any take.** Files are listed by scene, with search across every word said in them.
- **Comment on moments.** Pin a comment to a moment or a stretch of the cut, reply, and resolve it. Comments left on an earlier revision are labeled as such.
- **Chat with an agent.** Ask for changes in plain words. The built-in agent edits and renders with the same operations as the [API](/api-reference/introduction), the [CLI](/cli/index) and [MCP](/mcp).
- **Render with the price first.** The render sheet shows what each quality costs before you start.

## What you need

- **A Mac with macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later**, Apple silicon or Intel.
- **A VidJutsu Cloud account.** The editor doesn't work without one: all video work (uploads, storage, renders) runs on VidJutsu Cloud and is paid from your [wallet](/pricing). It uses the same API key as the CLI, so if you've run `vidjutsu login` you're already signed in. New here? [Sign up](/quickstart) first.
- **An AI model for chat**, any one of:
  - Sign in with ChatGPT.
  - Your own OpenAI API key.
  - Your own Anthropic API key.

  You don't need any of these to edit by hand.

</Tip>

## Install

1. [Download VidJutsu Editor](https://download.vidjutsu.ai/editor/VidJutsu-Editor.dmg).
2. Open the DMG and drag **VidJutsu Editor** to **Applications**.
3. Open it from Applications. It's signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without a warning.

The app updates itself: it checks once a day, and you can check any time with **Check for Updates…** in the app menu or the **Updates** tab in Settings.

Want a specific version? Each release is also published under its version number, for example [VidJutsu-Editor-0.1.0.dmg](https://download.vidjutsu.ai/editor/VidJutsu-Editor-0.1.0.dmg). [`latest.json`](https://download.vidjutsu.ai/editor/latest.json) gives the latest version, its SHA-256 and size.
