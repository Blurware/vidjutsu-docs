## Pro: $25 a month, plus usage

A card is required to start. There is no free plan.

| Usage | Included each month | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Render minutes, up to 1080p | 60 minutes | $0.10 per minute |
| Render minutes, above 1080p (4K) | None | $0.20 per minute |
| Storage | 100 GB | $0.05 per GB-month |

Render minutes are minutes of rendered output. Uploading is free; what you store counts as storage, billed on your average stored bytes over the month. A job that fails on our side is never charged. Usage is billed in arrears on your monthly invoice.

## Subscribe and manage billing

There is no billing UI of ours: every billing step is a Stripe-hosted page you get a link to.

| Call | CLI | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /billing/checkout` | `vidjutsu billing checkout` | A Stripe Checkout link that starts Pro. |
| `POST /billing/portal` | `vidjutsu billing portal` | A Stripe billing portal link: update your card, see invoices, cancel. |

Both take optional `https` redirect URLs (`success_url` and `cancel_url` for checkout, `return_url` for the portal). Your account turns `active` as soon as checkout completes.

Canceling in the portal ends your plan at the end of the billing period.

## When something needs fixing

Every billing error tells you how to fix it:

- **No plan:** work that needs a plan returns 402 `payment_required` with `reason: "no_plan"` and a `checkout_url`.
- **Failed payment:** new work stops at once with 402 `payment_required`, `reason: "payment_failed"` and a `portal_url` to update your card. Reads keep working so you can get your media out. The error and `GET /account` carry `media_deletes_at`: if the payment isn't made, stored media is deleted 30 days after the failed payment.
- **A plan limit:** 403 `plan_limit` with a `reason` (`spend_limit`, `cap_render_minutes`, `cap_storage`, `job_too_large`) and a `contact_url`.
- **Too many renders at once:** 429 `rate_limited` with `retry_after_seconds`.

See [Errors](/errors) for the full list.
