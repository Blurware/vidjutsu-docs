## Pay as you go

There is no monthly fee and no subscription. You add credits to a prepaid wallet, and every render and every stored GB is paid from it. To start, add a card and at least $5. Top up any time, $5 minimum. Your card is only charged for the payments you choose. There is no free plan.

| Usage | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Render minutes, up to 1080p | $0.10 per minute |
| Render minutes, above 1080p (4K) | $0.20 per minute |
| Storage | $0.05 per GB-month |

Render minutes are minutes of rendered output. A render holds its cost from your wallet when it starts; a render that fails on our side is never charged, and what it held goes back. Uploading is free. Storage is charged on your average stored bytes over the month.

### Limits

`GET /usage` (`vidjutsu usage`) shows your wallet balance and this month's usage and spend. `GET /entitlements` (`vidjutsu entitlements`) returns your limits.

## Fund your wallet

There is no billing UI of ours: every payment is a Stripe-hosted page you get a link to.

| Call | CLI | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POST /billing/checkout` | `vidjutsu billing checkout` | Sign up: add a card and make your first payment (default $5). |
| `POST /billing/top-up` | | Add credits any time (default $10). |
| `POST /billing/portal` | `vidjutsu billing portal` | Update your saved card or see your receipts. |

Checkout and top-up take an optional `amount_usd` in whole cents, from $5 to $500 per payment, for example `{"amount_usd": 25}`. All three take optional `https` redirect URLs (`success_url` and `cancel_url`, or `return_url` for the portal). Your account turns `active` as soon as the first payment goes through, and credits show up in `GET /usage` as soon as a top-up does.

<Note>
  CLI 2.1.0 has no top-up command yet. Call `POST /billing/top-up` directly, use the `billing_top_up` MCP tool, or call `request("POST", "/billing/top-up", {amount_usd: 20})` in the SDK.
</Note>

There is nothing to cancel. Stop using the service and you stop paying; storage is still charged while you keep media stored.

## When something needs fixing

Every billing error is a 402 `payment_required` that carries the link that fixes it:

- **Not signed up yet:** `reason: "no_plan"` and a `checkout_url` to add a card and fund your wallet.
- **Not enough credits:** a render your balance doesn't cover returns `reason: "insufficient_credits"` and a `top_up_url`. The message says what the render costs and what your wallet has.
- **Wallet below zero:** new work stops at once with `reason: "payment_failed"` and a `top_up_url`. Reads keep working so you can get your media out. The error and `GET /account` carry `media_deletes_at`: unless you top up, stored media is deleted 30 days after your wallet went below zero.

Other limits:

- **A plan limit:** 403 `plan_limit` with a `reason` (`spend_limit`, `cap_render_minutes`, `cap_storage`, `job_too_large`) and a `contact_url`.
- **Too many renders at once:** 429 `rate_limited` with `retry_after_seconds`.

See [Errors](/errors) for the full list.
