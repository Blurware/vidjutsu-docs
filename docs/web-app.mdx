VidJutsu is fully hosted. The editor runs in your browser at [app.vidjutsu.ai](https://app.vidjutsu.ai), and your media, projects, transcripts, Studio Sound and renders all run on VidJutsu Cloud. There is nothing to download or install, and nothing to set up besides your account.

You bring your agent; VidJutsu brings everything else. Point Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any MCP client at the hosted [MCP server](/mcp) and it edits the same projects you have open, while you see every change land as it happens, with Undo on each one.

## What it does

- **Plan with the shot list.** Lay out the video as scenes and shots, each with talking points, a rough script line and a key visual. See [Plan and record with the shot list](/guides/shot-list).
- **Record a scene or a shot.** Record your screen, camera and microphone right in Chrome. Screen and camera are saved as two files, and each take is filed under its scene as soon as you stop. Nothing goes on the timeline until you or your agent put it there.
- **Cut by the script.** Read the cut as a script next to the video. Select words and press Delete to cut them; pauses show in the script, so you can cut those too.
- **Edit on the timeline.** Play the cut, step through it frame by frame, and add, swap or remove clips.
- **Lay out screen and camera.** Pick a layout (a circle in the corner, side by side, camera only and more) for the selection or the whole video. See [Screen and camera layouts](/guides/layouts).
- **Studio Sound.** One switch cleans every voice in the project, with the price shown before you turn it on. See [Studio Sound](/guides/studio-sound).
- **Watch your agent work.** Activity shows each change as it lands, who made it, and what your agent says it's about to do. Undo any single change from History.
- **Comment on the picture.** Pin a comment to a moment and a spot on the video, reply and resolve it. An agent connected over [MCP](/mcp) reads every comment, makes the change and answers in the thread.
- **Render with the price first.** The render sheet shows what each quality costs before you start, and the finished video has a link you can share.

## What you need

- **Chrome on a computer.** Other browsers may work, but the app is made for Chrome for now.
- **A VidJutsu account.** All video work (uploads, storage, transcripts, Studio Sound and renders) runs on VidJutsu Cloud and is paid from your [wallet](/pricing). New here? Open [app.vidjutsu.ai](https://app.vidjutsu.ai) to sign up, or see the [quickstart](/quickstart).
- **Your agent, if you want one.** Connect it under **Connect agents** in the app, or see [MCP](/mcp). You don't need it to edit by hand.

You don't need accounts or API keys with any transcription, audio or rendering vendor. VidJutsu picks and runs those for you and bills the work from one wallet.
